Why it exists

LifeKindled helps families, churches, groups and communities strengthen connection and discipleship. It starts with one simple idea: Scripture is better read together.

Inside the app

Everything your group needs, built around the Bible.

Personal first, with community always one tap away. You can read on your own for as long as you like, and invite people in when you're ready.

Four translations

Read in NLT, NKJV, CSB or KJV, and switch whenever you want.

Reading plans

Follow the same plan as your group, one day at a time.

Groups

Be part of more than one, from youth group to small group.

Notes and highlights

Private by default. Shared only when you choose who sees them.

Prayer

Share prayer requests and lift each other up.

Friends

Add friends by scanning a QR code or searching a username.

Made for people who read together

Youth groups, college ministries, small groups, young adults, families and churches.
