Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026 · Applies to Aibram

Aibram is built by Eternity Works. This policy explains what information Aibram collects, why, and how it's used — written to reflect exactly what the app actually does.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google or with email and password (via Firebase Authentication), we receive your name and email address to create and manage your account.

Content you create. Tasks, calendar events, Space ideas, spending entries, goals, and conversations with Aibram are stored so the app can function. If you're an Aibram+ subscriber, this also includes Deep Memory (durable facts Aibram learns about you across conversations) and any documents or images you attach for Aibram to read.

Location. With your permission, Aibram uses your device's location to show local weather and to calculate real drive-time ("leave by") reminders for events. Location is used only when the app is open and only for these features — it is never collected in the background or stored as a location history.

Purchase information. Subscription purchases (Aibram+) are processed through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Apple and RevenueCat handle that directly.

Usage and advertising data. If you use the free version of Aibram, Google AdMob may collect device and usage information to serve ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy practices. Aibram+ subscribers see no ads and are not served ad-related tracking.

How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate the core features of the app (tasks, calendar, Focus, Space, spending, and the Aibram AI assistant)
  • Personalize Aibram's responses using the context of your own tasks, goals, and (for Aibram+ subscribers) Deep Memory
  • Process subscription purchases and restore them across your devices
  • Provide real drive-time reminders and local weather
  • Serve ads to free-tier users (via AdMob)

Aibram's AI features are powered by Anthropic's Claude models. Messages you send to Aibram are processed by Anthropic to generate a response; they are not used to train Anthropic's models beyond what Anthropic's own API terms specify.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers necessary to run the app:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and data storage
  • Anthropic — processing your messages to Aibram
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Google AdMob — serving ads to free-tier users
  • Google Maps Platform — drive-time calculations
  • Tavily — pulling local news for your Morning Brief

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing the data they process on our behalf.

Your Choices

You can delete your account at any time from Aibram's Profile screen (You → Delete Account), which permanently removes your account, stored content, and Deep Memory. You can also disable location permissions at any time in your device's Settings — Aibram will simply stop offering weather and drive-time features.

Children's Privacy

Aibram is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Aibram after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to eternityworks2026@gmail.com.
